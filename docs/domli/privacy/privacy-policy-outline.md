# Privacy policy outline (for domli.nl, have it reviewed by a privacy lawyer)

1. **Who we are**: Domli, KvK number, address, contact, privacy contact.
2. **Two roles**: for our own website and sales: we are the controller. For client conversations processed in the inbox: we are the **processor** for the client (see the verwerkersovereenkomst); the client is the controller.
3. **What we process** (own site): contact form data, account/billing data. (Inbox): messages, names, email, phone numbers, attachments, conversation metadata.
4. **Purposes and legal basis** (own site): contract, legitimate interest, legal obligation.
5. **Retention**: account data while the contract runs; conversation data per client instruction; backups kept 14 days; deletion at contract end within 30 days.
6. **Recipients**: subprocessors (link to subprocessors.md).
7. **Transfers outside the EEA**: only the optional AI provider, with safeguards; the client can disable it.
8. **Security**: TLS, encrypted channel credentials, access control, backups, logging policy (AI service logs token counts only, never message content).
9. **Rights**: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection. How to exercise (clients handle requests about their customers; we assist).
10. **Cookies** on domli.nl and in the dashboard (functional only, state it).
11. **Complaints**: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
12. **Changes** and date.

## Scope notes
- **Out of scope for the first version: healthcare and GP practices.** Medical data is special-category data (art. 9 AVG). Do not onboard such clients. The AI assistant hands off every health-related message to a human and never answers it automatically.
- Do not store identity documents or payment card data in conversations; tell clients.
